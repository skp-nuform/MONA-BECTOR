# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Executives and hiring leaders filling senior transformation roles: Director of Business Transformation,
Organizational Change Manager, Business Transformation Manager and Program Manager (the roles Mona lists as
"open to work" on LinkedIn). They arrive from a link (LinkedIn, an email, a referral), skim for fit in a minute
or two, and decide whether to start a conversation. Large public enterprises, including energy and
infrastructure organizations, are a likely employer context; the page nods to that only indirectly.

## Product Purpose

A one-page professional portfolio for Mona Bector, a digital transformation and change-management program
leader. Success: a hiring leader understands what she leads, sees the proof, and contacts her on LinkedIn.

## Positioning

25+ years across both public-sector leadership (New York State deputy commissioner; programs for the US EPA and
the Department of the Interior) and enterprise change (Leidos, BAE Systems, Pfizer, EY), with a recent focus on
AI adoption and stacked change-management credentials (PMP, MCMP, CCMP, Prosci, Change Management for
Generative AI). Works in English, Hindi and Urdu.

## Operating Context

Read on a laptop between meetings or on a phone from a LinkedIn message. The one action is "Contact me on
LinkedIn" (https://www.linkedin.com/in/monabector/). No email address or phone number is published.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: Astro static site (`src/pages/index.astro`), assets in `public/`.
- First person, Mona's voice.
- GAIL (India) Ltd is not named; at most an indirect nod to energy and infrastructure.
- Undecided: a canonical domain.

## Evidence on Hand

- LinkedIn profile (read 2026-11-18): About text, ten roles 2020–2026 with descriptions, ten certifications,
  five languages.
- Press (Portland Press Herald, 2018): Deputy Commissioner of Workforce and Talent Management at New York State
  OPWDD, 2014–2016; master's in international business (Nyenrode); bachelor's in education (CUNY).
- Photographs: Wikimedia Commons stand-ins in `public/assets/img/` with `credits.json`.
- Absent and never to be invented: testimonials, client quotes, metrics or outcomes beyond the profile text,
  employer logos, a portrait (the user will supply one later; a portrait slot holds a stand-in until then).

## Product Principles

1. Every claim traces to her LinkedIn or the press record.
2. Proof before adjectives: roles, organizations, years and credentials do the persuading.
3. One clear action, repeated sparingly: contact on LinkedIn.
4. Senior and calm, never salesy; lively through interaction, not noise.
